# Ethics and privacy

Prohori is a software demonstration using authored synthetic fixtures and a
public synthetic SMART sandbox. This work does not recruit participants or
collect identifiable patient data. No ethics-board approval is claimed, and
these demonstrations do not establish approval for any future clinical study.

Do not enter real names, National IDs, patient records or credentials into
public FHIR sandboxes or this demo. Repository NID-shaped identifiers and login
credentials are synthetic. Test evidence excludes access tokens and secrets.

The dashboard is not validated for clinical decisions or public-health burden
estimation. Any future deployment using real health data requires a separate
governance assessment, appropriate permissions and operational safeguards.
